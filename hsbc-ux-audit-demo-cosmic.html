How it works

The 3-step guide.

01

Upload

Drop a screenshot, source file, or design file. The agent reads what's there — no live URL needed.

02

Run audit

The engine checks 18 self-audit items across four areas — Customer Mindset, UX, UI, Copy — and scores each.

03

Review

See a UX score (out of 100), a Pass / Not-pass verdict, and a punch list of action items ready for PR or Jira.

Scoring

Each of the four areas has between 4 and 5 self-audit items. Every item resolves to one of three states after the audit runs:

Pass — item detected in the uploaded file.
Review — ambiguous evidence; needs a human to confirm.
Missing — not present or not detected; turns into an action item.

Each category scores passes / total × 100. The overall UX score is the average of the four category scores. 70 is the pass threshold — anything below is "Not passed" and needs the missing items addressed before review.
